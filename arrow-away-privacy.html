Privacy Policy — Arrow Away - Puzzle Escape

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Arrow Away - Puzzle Escape ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by DevTech Labs, operates the Arrow Away mobile application. This Privacy Policy informs users of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information for users of our app.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, address, phone number, or email address directly. The app has no accounts and no sign-in. Your game progress, scores, stars, achievements and settings are stored only on your device.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Our app uses third-party advertising services that may collect information used to identify your device for ad serving, personalization, fraud prevention and measurement:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob (used for serving ads): AdMob may use device advertising identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID) to serve relevant ads. For more information, visit Google Advertising & Privacy.
  • Unity Ads (used as a backup ad network): Unity may collect device information and the device advertising identifier to serve and measure ads. For more information, visit Unity's Privacy Policy FAQ.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads), and you can opt out of ad personalization there as well.

3. Cookies and Tracking

The advertising services above may use cookies or similar technologies to improve ad relevance and analyze app traffic. The app itself does not use cookies.

4. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Game data stored on your device is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Data collected by the advertising partners listed above is retained according to their own privacy policies.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
