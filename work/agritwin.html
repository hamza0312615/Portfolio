Field Shift — farms & NASA data

The challenge

Farmers across Pakistan plant and irrigate by habit and guesswork while free NASA satellite data — soil moisture, rainfall, vegetation health — sits unused, locked in formats no farmer can read. The NASA Space Apps challenge "Field Shift" asked: adapt farms with NASA data. My team had one hackathon weekend.

What I built

AgriTwin takes a farmer’s field location and turns satellite signals into plain decisions: where the soil is dry, where crops are stressed, and what to do next. The interface is built for non-technical users first — big, clear, and honest about what the data can and cannot say.

The result

AgriTwin was built and demoed for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, Islamabad, under Team DevTech. It shows the same skill I sell: taking complex data and AI, and shipping a tool a busy person can actually use.
