VisionDX Mega: 12 AI health tools, one platform

Project
VisionDX Mega
My role
Designer & developer
Stack
React · FastAPI · MediaPipe
Result
Top 12 — Google AI Seekho 2026

The challenge

In Pakistan, basic health screening often means long travel and long waits — and for deaf patients, even a simple consultation can fail at the first sentence. The challenge: build one platform that gives people fast, understandable first-step health guidance, in their own language, even on a weak connection.

What I built

VisionDX Mega brings 12 AI diagnostic tools together: image and report analysis, a symptom checker, OCR for prescriptions, and drug verification against openFDA data. Inside the same platform sits a full Pakistani Sign Language translator (MediaPipe hand tracking) and a WhatsApp triage bot, with a Community Health Worker mode and a 6-language interface designed to keep working offline.

The result

VisionDX Mega reached the national finals of Google AI Seekho 2026 (Top 12) and finished Top 10 in the GDG IST Innovators Challenge. It is live, public, and still growing — the same engineering approach I bring to client web apps and AI tools.
