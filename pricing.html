Pricing questions

Before you ask

How do payments work?

Order through Fiverr or Upwork, where your payment stays protected until delivery, or agree milestones on WhatsApp. The fixed quote is agreed in writing before work starts.

What if I need changes after launch?

Every package includes revision rounds before launch and a support window after it. Bigger changes later are quoted separately, again in writing.

Are there any extra costs?

Your domain and any paid third-party services (for example premium plugins or API usage) are yours and paid by you directly. I will tell you about them in the quote before we start — no surprises.

Can I start small and upgrade later?

Yes. Many clients start with a launch site or a starter agent and add more once it is working. Your build is made to grow.
