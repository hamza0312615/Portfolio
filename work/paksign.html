PakSign: sign language becomes text & speech, live

Project
PakSign Translator
My role
Designer & developer
Stack
MediaPipe Hands · React
Modes
Alphabet + word signs

The challenge

Pakistan has a large deaf community, but almost no everyday tools that translate Pakistani Sign Language for hearing people. A deaf person at a clinic, a bank or a school office faces a wall of silence. The challenge: real-time translation that runs in a normal browser, on a normal phone camera.

What I built

PakSign uses MediaPipe hand tracking to read hand shapes and movement, and turns them into text and spoken words — both single alphabet signs and full word signs. It needs no special hardware: open the page, allow the camera, and start signing.

The result

The translator is live and public, and its technology also lives inside VisionDX Mega, where it helps deaf patients communicate symptoms. It is the clearest example of what I build: AI that removes a real barrier for real people.
