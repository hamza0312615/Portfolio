Questions

Before you ask

How is an agent different from a chatbot?

A chatbot only talks. An agent also acts: it captures the lead, books the slot, sends the follow-up. Talking is one of its tools, not the whole job.

Do I need a new website for an agent?

No. Agents can be added to most existing websites, and connected to your forms and WhatsApp.

What does the agent need from me?

Your service list, prices and common questions — one short chat is enough for me to train it.
